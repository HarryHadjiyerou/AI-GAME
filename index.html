Touch — right thumb steers (gentle pitch; push forward to nose down). Left thumb: hold FLAP to climb, hold DIVE to tuck and plunge (let go to swoop back up), hold BOOST for a burst of speed. Flick the stick hard left or right and let go for a barrel roll.

Keyboard — WASD / arrows steer · Space flap · C dive · Shift boost · Q / E barrel roll · P pause

Fun — ride the updraft on the windward side of cliffs and ridges (look for rising motes and listen for the beeper), then fold into a free-fall. Fly low through canyons, between trunks and under titan branches. Chase the glowing rings for speed.
