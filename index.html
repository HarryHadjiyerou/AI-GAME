Controls

Phone

  • Left stick — steer. Push to bank, pull back to raise the nose.
  • Right stick — look around without changing course
  • FLAP — a wingbeat. Hold for several.
  • DIVE — fold the wings and drop

Both sticks are floating — they appear wherever your thumb lands, so you never have to look for them.

Desktop

  • A / D or ← → — bank
  • W / S or ↑ ↓ — nose down / nose up
  • Space — flap · Shift — dive · Mouse drag — look
  • Esc — menu · P — hide interface · F — performance

A game controller works too: left stick steers, right stick looks, A or right trigger beats.

How to actually fly

  • Let go of the stick and the bird settles into a glide. It will not dive at the ground on its own.
  • Banking is how you turn. Lean the stick over and the turn happens by itself.
  • Height is speed and speed is height. Dive to go fast, pull up to trade it back.
  • Rising air is marked by spiralling motes. Circle inside one and you go up without beating.
