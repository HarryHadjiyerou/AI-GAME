Controls

Phone — tilt

  • Tilt left / right — bank and turn
  • Tilt forward — dive · tilt back — climb
  • Tap anywhere — flap
  • Hold — tuck wings (dive)
  • Two-finger tap — re-centre the tilt neutral point

iOS asks permission for motion sensors — the prompt appears when you take flight.

Touch — drag fallback

  • Drag — steer (used automatically when no tilt sensor)
  • Tap — flap · hold — tuck

Desktop

  • A / D or ← → — bank
  • W / S or ↑ ↓ — dive / climb
  • Space — flap · Shift — tuck · Mouse drag — look
  • Esc — menu · P — photo mode · F — perf readout
